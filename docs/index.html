▸ Options
01

Set your pace.

Choose 10–40 characters per second, or try experimental instant mode to send text in quick bursts.

02

Keep your spacing.

Optional indentation correction clears spaces your editor adds after Enter, then enters your original whitespace.

03

Stay in control.

Set a 1–60 second countdown to give yourself time to switch apps. Press Escape to stop. Your text stays on your Mac.

THREE QUICK STEPS

Drag. Open. Allow.

  1. Open the DMG and drag AutoType to Applications.
  2. Allow AutoType in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.
  3. Enter your text, click Send, and focus your destination.
A note on compatibility. AutoType sends keyboard events; it does not invoke clipboard paste. Instant mode is experimental, and receiving apps may change or reject input. Indentation correction requires the macOS Command-Shift-Left shortcut. Start on an empty line when using it.
